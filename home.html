Why CardioSense

A risk model built around women's bodies

Most cardiovascular risk tools were developed and validated on predominantly male datasets. CardioSense was built differently from the ground up.

Biological differences, accounted for

Women often present with different symptoms and risk pathways than men — from how cholesterol and blood pressure interact to inflammatory and metabolic markers. CardioSense weighs these factors the way the underlying data actually shows they behave.

Post-menopausal risk, built in

Estrogen decline after menopause is one of the sharpest inflection points in a woman's cardiovascular risk. Menopausal status, hysterectomy, and related history are first-class inputs — not an afterthought.

Grounded in real clinical data

Trained on NHANES — the CDC's national health and nutrition survey — combining lab-measured biomarkers with reproductive health and medical history, filtered specifically to female respondents.

Under the hood

Transparent by design

CardioSense doesn't just hand you a score. Every prediction comes with an explanation, and nothing you enter is stored.

Read the full methodology
Explainable results Each prediction is broken down into the top factors increasing or decreasing your risk, powered by SHAP.
Nothing is stored Your responses are processed in memory to generate a result and are never saved to a database.
Calibrated probabilities Risk scores are calibrated so a given probability reflects real-world likelihood, not just a raw model score.
Free and instant No sign-up, no payment, no waiting room — get a result in about the time it takes to fill out the form.

CardioSense is a screening and education tool, not a diagnostic device. It does not replace professional medical advice — always talk to a doctor about your cardiovascular health.
